# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project is intended to be a respectful, public-demo software repository. Contributors and maintainers should use welcoming, professional language and focus critique on the work.

## Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful of differing experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Keep discussions relevant to the software, documentation, and demo goals.
- Avoid language that implies real people, veterans, providers, or organizations committed fraud, waste, or abuse.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information or sensitive data.
- Introducing real PHI, PII, claim, patient, veteran, VA internal, or government data.
- Presenting demo risk indicators as confirmed misconduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in blocked participation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, documentation, discussions, and any project-related communication channel.
