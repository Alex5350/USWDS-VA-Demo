# Case Assistant Sample Questions

Use these prompts to demonstrate grouping, summarization, and operational analysis against the synthetic case dataset.

1. Give me an executive summary of the current case inventory grouped by status, risk level, and estimated questioned cost.

2. Treat open cases as New, UnderReview, Referred, and Escalated. How many open cases are there, and how are they distributed by status?

3. Which provider types account for the most estimated questioned cost? Group by provider type and call out the top contributors.

4. Summarize the critical-risk cases by status, provider type, and estimated questioned cost. Which ones should get the most attention first?

5. Which providers have the highest combination of critical/high-risk cases and estimated questioned cost? Give me the top 10 with a short rationale.

6. Show me the aging profile for active cases. Which statuses have the most cases older than 31 days and 61 days?

7. Compare Dental, Imaging, and Durable Medical Equipment cases by volume, risk level, and estimated questioned cost.

8. Group cases by assignee and summarize workload, risk mix, and estimated questioned cost. Who appears to have the heaviest queue?

9. Find the highest-risk New cases and summarize the common risk flags, providers, procedure codes, and estimated questioned cost.

10. Create a supervisor briefing: what are the main patterns in the risk queue, which providers or provider types stand out, and what follow-up questions should an analyst ask next?
